Get Involved

This project is an open, ongoing effort to build a low-cost, DIY assistive mobility aid for blind and low-vision users. If you are an orientation-and-mobility instructor, an accessibility researcher, or a maker who wants to help test or extend the design — get in touch or open an issue on GitHub.
